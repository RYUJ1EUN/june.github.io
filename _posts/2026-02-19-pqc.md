#### Game-based Security

비밀 정보를 지닌 Challenger와 그 비밀을 탈취하려는 Attacker 간의 싸움을 모델링한 것

##### Attacker (Adversary)

- 공개 정보 획득 가능

- 공격자의 능력이 특정 오라클 형태로 제한됨
- 공격자의 모든 행동은 다항식 시간 알고리즘으로 표현되어야 함


##### PKE/KEM 안전성 정의

- 공격자는 특정 메시지에 대한 캡슐화를 할 수 있음
- 공격자는 특정 암호문에 대한 복호화를
    - 할 수 없음: IND-CPA
    - 할 수 있음: IND-CCA1
    - 암호문을 활용하여 암호화, 복호화를 할 수 있음: IND-CCA2
- $\text{Adv}^{\text{IND-win}}({\cal A}) \ge \frac{1}{2}$일 때 안전하다고 함
    - $\text{Adv}^{\text{IND}}({\cal A}) = \Pr[\text{Exp}^{\text{IND-1}}(A)=1]-\Pr[\text{Exp}^{\text{IND-0}}({\cal A})=1]$
    - $\Pr[\text{Exp}^{\text{IND-1}}({\cal A})=1]-\Pr[\text{Exp}^{\text{IND-0}}({\cal A})=1] < \epsilon$ (공격자가 0,1을 구별할 수 없음)
    - $\text{Adv}^{\text{IND-win}}({\cal A}) = \frac{1}{2}\left( \text{Adv}^{\text{IND}}({\cal A}) +1\right)$



##### 전자서명의 안전성 정의

- 공격자는 특정 서명에 대한 검증을 요청할 수 있음
- 공격자는 특정 메시지에 대한 서명을 
    - 요청할 수 없음: UF-NMA
    - 요청할 수 있음: UF-CMA
    - 동일 메시지에 대한 반복 서명을 요청할 수 있음: SUF-CMA


#### Lattice

- $n$-차원 격자: discrete additive subgroup of $\mathbb{R}^n$
- periodic grid

##### 주요 격자 문제

- SIVP (Shortest Independent Vectors Problem)
- **SVP** (Shortest Vector Problem)
- CVP (Closest Vector Problem)
- uSVP (unique SVP)

##### SVP

- 가장 짧은 0이 아닌 격자 벡터는?
- $\text{SVP}\_\gamma$
    - 적당히($\gamma$) 짧은 0이 아닌 격자 벡터는? 
    - 즉, 가장 짧은 벡터의 크기가 $\lambda_1$일 때, $0<\|v\|\le\gamma\cdot\lambda_1$인 $v$를 찾는 문제
    - 특정 범위의 작은 $\gamma$에 대해 NP-Hard

##### SIS (Short Integer Solution)

- $\mathbb{Z}\_q$ 상의 선형 방정식의 짧은 벡터 해 찾기
- 행렬 $A\in{\mathbb{Z}\_q}^{n\times m}$를 기반으로 격자 생성 가능  
    $${\mathcal{L}_{q}}^\perp (A) := \{ \vec{z}\in\mathbb{Z}^m \mid Az=0 \mod q \}$$
- 랜덤 행렬 $A$에 대한 SIS 문제의 난이도가 가장 어려운 SIVP 문제의 난이도보다 높음

##### LWE (Learning With Errors)

- 작은 error를 `Gaussian dist.에서 랜덤하게 선택한 error` 포함한 연립선형방정식의 해를 구하는 문제
- error $e$를 포함하게 만든 $(A,b=Ax+e)$와 랜덤하게 생성한 $(A,b)$를 구별하기 어려움
    $${\mathcal{L}_q}^\perp (A) := \{ \vec{z}\in\mathbb{Z}^m \mid z^t=s^t A \mod q \}$$
- LWR (Learning With Rounding): 반올힘한 error를 사용
- MLWE (Module LWE): 다항식 환 위에서 정의


##### 격자기반 난제의 난이도

- dimension $n$이 클수록 어려움
- modulus $q$가 작을수록 어려움
- (Gap)SVP: $\gamma$가 작을수록 어려움
- SIS: 해의 크기가 작을수록 어려움
- LWE: 비밀키/오류 크기가 클수록 어려움

#### Security of DSA

Informal Meaning  
"If you sign a message honestly, the verifier will always accept it."

**Formal Definition**  
A signature scheme (KeyGen, Sign, Verify) is correct if for all security parameters $\lambda$, all messages $m$, and all key pairs ($sk, vk$) output by KeyGen($\lambda$):
$$
Pr\left[\text{Verify}(vk, m, \text{Sign}(sk, m)) = 1\right] = 1.
$$
For schemes with negligible error, the probability may be $\ge 1 - \text{negl}(\lambda)$, not exactly 1.  
What Correctness Does NOT GuaranteeIt says nothing about forged signatures being rejected.  
Security (unforgeability) is a separate property.



##### EUF-CMA Security: Formal Game Definition

(Informal) Existential Unforgeability against Chosen Message Attacks (EUF-CMA)
- An attacker who can get signatures on messages of their choice cannot forge a valid signature on any new message.
- Most digital signature schemes aim for this level of security.

**Definition (EUF-CMA Security)**
- A signature scheme (KeyGen, Sign, Verify) is existentially unforgeable under chosen-message attacks (EUF-CMA) if for every PPT adversary $\mathcal{A}$, the adversary's winning probability in the following game is negligible in the security parameter $\lambda$:
1. Setup: The challenger runs $(vk, sk) \leftarrow \text{KeyGen}(\lambda)$ and gives $vk$ to $\mathcal{A}$.
2. Query Phase: $\mathcal{A}$ adaptively queries a signing oracle on messages $m_i$ of its choice and receives $\sigma_i \leftarrow \text{Sign}(sk, m_i)$ for $1 \le i \le q$.
3. Forgery: $\mathcal{A}$ outputs a pair $(m^*, \sigma^*)$.The adversary wins if $\text{Verify}(vk, m^*, \sigma^*) = 1$ and $m^* \notin \{m_1, \dots, m_q\}$.


##### Stronger Security Notion: Strong Unforgeability

**Strong Unforgeability under Chosen Message Attack (SUF-CMA)**  
The adversary wins if it outputs $(m^*, \sigma^*)$ such that
$$
\text{Verify}(vk, m^*, \sigma^*) = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad (m^*, \sigma^*) \notin \{(m_i, \sigma_i)\}.
$$
- Key difference from EUF-CMA: Even generating a different valid signature for a previously signed message is considered a forgery.  
`같은 메시지를 여러 차례 전송하는 경우, 메시지에 deterministic하게 서명이 생성된다면 이전에 전송된 서명을 복사하여 사용할 수 있다는 문제가 발생함`


#### DSA using Error Correcting Codes

$$
\text{sender } \mathbf{c} \xrightarrow{\quad \text{noise } \mathbf{e} \quad} \mathbf{s} = \mathbf{c} + \mathbf{e} \text{ receiver}
$$
- $\mathcal{C}$: a linear code of length $n$ and dimension $k$  
    $\Rightarrow$ a $k$-dimensional subspace of $\mathbb{F}_q^n$  
    e.g., Hamming codes, Reed-Solomon codes, Golay codes, Goppa codes and so on.
- a generator matrix G for a code $\mathcal{C}$: a $k \times n$ matrix such that
    $$
    \mathcal{C} = \{ \mathbf{m}_{1\times k} \cdot \mathbf{G}_{k\times n} : \mathbf{m} \in \mathbb{F}_q^k \}
    $$
- a parity check matrix H: a matrix such that $\mathbf{H} \cdot \mathbf{G}^T = 0$ (and so $\mathbf{H} \cdot \mathbf{c}^T = 0$ for all codewords $\mathbf{c}$)
    $Hs = H(c+e) = Hc + He = He$ `secure for small s and random H`
- We can easily compute a $(n - k) \times n$ parity check matrix $\mathbf{H}$ from given $\mathbf{G}$ using Gaussian elimination.


##### How to show the Signing algorithm is Hard?

(전제) 서명 알고리즘 $S$가 안전하지 않으면 hard problem $P$를 풀 수 있다.
→ (대우) $P$를 풀 수 없으면 $S$가 안전하다.

**The Hard Problem: Syndrome Decoding (SD)**  
Given:
- Matrix H of size $(n-k) \times n$ over $\mathbb{F}_2$
- Target syndrome vector $\mathbf{s}^T = \mathbf{H} \cdot \mathbf{e}^T$
- Target weight threshold $w$ `w 조건이 없을 땐 쉬운 문제인데, w가 주어지면 어려운 문제가 됨`  
Find: Error vector e with $wt(\mathbf{e}) \leq w$, where $\mathbf{H} \cdot \mathbf{e}^T = \mathbf{s}^T \pmod 2$. `H, s가 공개된 상태에서 w라는 제약이 있을 때 He=s를 만족하는 e를 찾는 것이 어려움`



#### Key Idea of CFS Signature
CFS Signature: A representative code-based signature scheme  
- N. Courtois, M. Finiasz, and N. Sendrier, How to achieve a McEliece-based digital signature scheme, Asiacrypt 2001.

**Key Idea**  
- Design a signature scheme based on the hardness of the SD problem
$$
\underbrace{\mathbf{H}}_{\text{verification key } vk} \cdot \underbrace{\mathbf{e}^T}_{\text{signature}} = \underbrace{\mathcal{H}(m)}_{\text{hash of message}} \text{ with } wt(\mathbf{e}) \text{ small}
$$
- For a random matrix H, finding a low-weight solution e is hard.
- Use a Goppa code parity-check matrix $\mathbf{H}_G$ with trapdoor decoding capability, concealed via suitable transformations. `Hg를 가진 사람만 해를 구하는 것이 쉬움`
$$
\text{verification key: } \mathbf{H} = \mathbf{S} \cdot \mathbf{H}_G \cdot \mathbf{P}
$$
- $\mathbf{S}$: a $(n - k) \times (n - k)$ invertible matrix, $\mathbf{P}$: a $n \times n$ permutation matrix


##### Description of CFS Signature
- KeyGen
1. Generate a Goppa-code parity check matrix $\mathbf{H}_G \in \mathbb{F}_2^{(n-k) \times n}$.
2. Generate random $(n-k) \times (n-k)$ invertible matrix $\mathbf{S}$ and $n \times n$ permutation matrix $\mathbf{P}$.
3. $vk = \mathbf{H} (= \mathbf{S} \cdot \mathbf{H}_G \cdot \mathbf{P}) \in \mathbb{F}_2^{(n-k) \times n}$ and $sk = (\mathbf{S}, \mathbf{H}' , \mathbf{P})$.

- SignCompute 
1. $\mathbf{x} = \text{D}_{\mathbf{H}'}(\mathbf{S}^{-1} \cdot \mathcal{H}(M \| \alpha))$ until $\mathbf{x}$ is found for $\alpha \overset{\$}{\leftarrow} \mathbb{F}_2^{n-k}$.
2. Output $(\alpha, \mathbf{s})$ where $\mathbf{s} = \mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{P}$.

- Verify
1. Output 1 iff $wt(\mathbf{s}) \le t$ and $\mathbf{H} \cdot \mathbf{s}^T = \mathcal{H}(M \| \alpha)$.  
    $(\because \mathbf{H} \cdot \mathbf{s}^T = (\mathbf{S} \cdot \mathbf{H}' \cdot \mathbf{P})(\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{P})^T = \mathbf{S} \cdot \mathbf{H}' \cdot \mathbf{x}^T = \mathbf{S} \cdot \mathbf{S}^{-1} \cdot \mathcal{H}(M \| \alpha))$



##### Security of CFS Signature
- Security relies on the hardness of the Syndrome Decoding (SD) problem:
    - Given $\mathbf{H} \in \mathbb{F}_2^{(n-k) \times n}$ and $\mathbf{y}$, find $\mathbf{s}$ such that $\mathbf{H} \cdot \mathbf{s}^T = \mathbf{y}$ and $wt(\mathbf{s}) \le t$.
- If $H$ is uniformly random, finding such a low-weight s is computationally hard.
- In CFS, however, $H$ has special structure ($\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{S} \cdot \mathbf{H}_G \cdot \mathbf{P}$). To argue security, we additionally assume that the decisional McEliece problem defined below is computationally hard.  
    This problem is also known as the Distinguishing Goppa Code problem.

**Decisional McEliece Problem: DMcE($n, k, t$)**  
Given $\mathbf{H} \in \mathbb{F}_2^{(n-k) \times n}$, distinguish whether
- $H$ is uniformly random over $\mathbb{F}_2^{(n-k) \times n}$, or
- $\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{S} \cdot \mathbf{H}_G \cdot \mathbf{P}$ for a hidden Goppa code.


- Goal: Find $s$ where $H s = y$
- Given $(S_i, y_i)$ pairs satisfying $H s_i = y_i$
