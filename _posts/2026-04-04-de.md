##### General form of D.E.

$$F(x, y, y',...,y^{(n)}) = 0 \quad\cdots\text{(DE)}$$


### 1st Order Differential Equations

1. There is no general method for solving DE.
2. Several methods are effective for a subclass of them.
    - separable equation, integrating factor, exact form, etc
3. Numerical approximation can be obtained by Euler’s method.

##### Slope field and Solution Curve

Consider the 1st order DE defined as (normal form of DE)

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = f (x, y).$$

$\frac{dy}{dx}$ is a slope of $(x, y)$ of the target line.

The solution of it is a curve in the xy-plane whose tangent line at each point
has slope $m = f (x, y)$.


**Slope field(directional field)**

- At each point $(x, y)$, the value of $f (x, y)$ determines a slope $m = f (x, y)$ of the tangent line.
- Short line segments having the slope $m = f (x, y)$ constitute a slope field(or directional field) for the equation $y'= f(x,y)$.
